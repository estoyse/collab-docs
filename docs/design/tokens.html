Presence — and --self

Eight hues at identical lightness (≈0.55) and chroma (≈0.094) in OKLCH. Vary only the hue and no collaborator's cursor can shout over another's. Whichever of these eight a person is hashed into also becomes --self on their own screen — the colour on your cursor is the colour your own interface is drawn in.

Type

Two variable families with one job each. Hanken Grotesk runs every control and label; Literata appears only inside the document, where people read rather than operate. Eight sizes: six on a 12/14/16/20/28/40 scale, plus a 17px prose size and an 11px caret-label size.

Controls

Base UI primitives with our tokens over them, in the shadcn file layout. Radius tops out at 6px (three sizes: 3/4/6px, every larger Tailwind step clamped down to 6px), borders are ink at 11%, and there is one focus treatment across every primitive: a 2px --self outline, offset from the control.

The warning that matters most

Offline editing is unavailable in this browser session, so changes made without a connection will not be kept. Private browsing windows and blocked site data are the usual causes.

The document

Everything above exists to keep this quiet. The header is slim and borderless, the title lives on the page itself, and on wide screens the toolbar is a sticky vertical rail beside it rather than a ribbon above it. The only colour on screen belongs to the people writing with you.

Quarterly review

We shipped the sync layer in six weeks, which is two weeks longer than planned and entirely accounted for by the offline work. That trade was worth making: the reconnection path is the part customers notice, and it is now the part we trust most.

What changed

Edits made without a connection are held locally and merged on reconnectDilnoza, with no version to pick between and nothing overwritten.

  • Documents open from local storage before the network is consulted.
  • Concurrent edits to the same paragraph converge rather than conflicting.Marat
  • Closing the last tab no longer risks the document.
